# v1.7.0 — UPKP 2026 Scope Alignment

Primary source reviewed: [Portal Resmi UPKP 2026](https://sites.google.com/view/upkp2026/beranda), checked 9 November 2026. The portal links to separate Petunjuk Teknis and Konversi Nilai TPA pages, but those linked pages were not accessible through the current retrieval path during this review.

## Scope confirmed on the official portal page

- **Tes Potensi:** Verbal, Numerikal, Figural.
- **TSKKWK:** Etika Pegawai Negeri Sipil; Nilai-Nilai Kementerian Keuangan; Tata Aturan Kepegawaian; Pengelolaan Keuangan Negara; Wawasan Kebangsaan.

The portal page reviewed does not provide a detailed per-topic blueprint, subtopic weighting, question count allocation, or a TPA raw-to-scaled-score conversion table. The page is titled UPKP 2026, but Section D's body text unexpectedly refers to the 2025 examination year. The linked Juknis should be checked before treating the detailed topic split or test structure as definitive.

## App treatment

- TPA is grouped under the three official subtests. Logical reasoning continues as a trained skill within the verbal learning path, not as a fourth official subtest.
- TSKKWK practice, mock-test generation, adaptive focus, and readiness badges use only the five topics explicitly listed on the portal page.
- Structure Kementerian Keuangan remains in Course Library as **Pengayaan Coach**. It is available for optional learning but is excluded from official-scope mixed practice, mock-test allocation, and TSKKWK readiness badges.
- TSKKWK mock-test distribution of four questions per topic is an internal balanced practice choice, not an official weight or blueprint.
- The psychometric track is intentionally not included in this release's learning plan, diagnostic, or readiness model per product scope.

## TPA score interpretation

UPKP Coach must not equate a learner's raw accuracy or percent-correct with the official TPA scaled score. Until the official Konversi Nilai TPA method/table is accessible and verified, the app reports practice accuracy and subtest-level evidence only. It does not generate a predicted official score.

Recommended future implementation once the official conversion guide is available:
1. Implement the published conversion exactly, preserving any subtest-specific rules, rounding, and version/period conditions.
2. Keep raw correct count, percent-correct, and converted official-equivalent score as separate fields.
3. Display a converted score only when the official conversion inputs are complete; never infer missing inputs from an overall accuracy.
4. Keep practice estimates distinct from actual official results and store the conversion-method version alongside each result.
