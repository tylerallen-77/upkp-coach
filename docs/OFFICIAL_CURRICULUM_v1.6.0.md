# v1.6.0 — Official Curriculum Alignment + Deep Course

Primary authority: ND-1188/PP.7/2026 dated 29 September 2026.

## Official user-facing nomenclature
- Tes Potensi: Verbal, Numerikal, Figural.
- TSKKWK: Tes Substansi Kementerian Keuangan dan Wawasan Kebangsaan.
- Tes Psikologi remains a separate official exam material category.

Logical reasoning remains a trained micro-skill but is not presented as a fourth official Tes Potensi subtest. Its evidence contributes to Verbal mastery.

## TSKKWK scope honesty
The UPKP notice names TSKKWK but does not publish a six-subtest blueprint. UPKP Coach therefore labels Etika ASN, Wawasan Kebangsaan, Nilai Kemenkeu, Kepegawaian, Keuangan Negara, and Struktur Kemenkeu as internal learning domains, not official subtests.

## Deep Course
All 16 Tes Potensi chapters and 6 TSKKWK learning domains now add bimbel-style instruction on top of the core notes: thinking model, step-by-step method, guided example, common traps, and exam target. Reading itself is not counted as mastery; learning evidence continues to come from recall and attempts.
