## Scope used by the app

### Tes Potensi

The official homepage lists three subtests: Verbal, Numerikal, and Figural. UPKP Coach maps its practice chapters into these three groups. Logical reasoning is a trained skill within the verbal path, not a fourth official subtest.

The homepage reviewed does not publish detailed per-chapter topic weights, time allocations, or item counts. Any chapter-level mix or mock-test distribution in UPKP Coach is an internal practice design.

### TSKKWK

The homepage lists five subject areas:

1. Etika Pegawai Negeri Sipil
2. Nilai-Nilai Kementerian Keuangan
3. Tata Aturan Kepegawaian
4. Pengelolaan Keuangan Negara
5. Wawasan Kebangsaan

The app uses these five topics in adaptive mixed practice, mock-test generation, and readiness badges. Mock-test allocation is four practice questions per topic; this is an internal balanced design, not an official exam weighting.

"Struktur Kementerian Keuangan" remains available in Course Library as optional Pengayaan Coach. It is excluded from required mixed practice, mock-test generation, and the TSKKWK readiness calculation because it does not appear in the five-topic list on the homepage reviewed.

### Current app scope

The learning and readiness flow in this release focuses on Tes Potensi and TSKKWK. Psychotest is intentionally outside this scope. Existing regulation/source links are shown within the lesson detail, with the official UPKP portal and Juknis links included as primary references.

## TPA score conversion policy

A learner's number correct, percentage accuracy, and internal practice progress are not the official scaled TPA score. UPKP Coach must keep those values separate.

No predicted official TPA score is shown until the official conversion page/table is accessible and its rules can be verified. Once available, implement the official conversion exactly, including any subtest-specific requirements, rounding, and period/version conditions. Preserve the raw number correct, percentage correct, converted result, and conversion-method version separately. Never estimate missing inputs from overall accuracy.

The app therefore currently uses diagnostic accuracy and learner skill evidence for coaching only; it does not claim these are a converted official TPA score.
