# UPKP Coach — Final Rebuild Specification

## Product goal
UPKP Coach is not a question bank. It is a guided exam coach whose job is to reduce uncertainty:
1. tell the learner what to do today,
2. observe accuracy, speed, confidence, retention and error patterns,
3. concentrate practice on the highest-value gaps,
4. prove section readiness through Mastery Badges.

## Main learner navigation
Only four primary surfaces:
- Beranda
- Belajar
- Try Out
- Progress

Advanced mechanics remain behind the scenes.

## Adaptive allocation
Target behavior:
- ~70% weaknesses / review-due / high-ROI gaps
- ~20% maintenance of already strong areas
- ~10% stretch transfer using harder archetypes

## Difficulty
1. Foundation — direct, one-step, familiar pattern.
2. Standard — normal practice; 1–2 reasoning steps.
3. Exam — timed exam-like item with meaningful distractors.
4. Hard — less obvious pattern, multi-step, transfer/information noise.
5. Expert — optional ceiling challenge; not required for mastery.

Hard != bigger numbers. Difficulty must come from reasoning depth, obscurity, distractors, information selection, or combined concepts.

## Mastery principle
A badge is evidence, not decoration. Easy-question farming must not award mastery.

Main TPA badges:
- Verbal Mastery
- Numerical Mastery
- Logical Mastery
- Figural Mastery

TPA Ready is shown only when all four are earned.

## Mastery gate
A section badge requires:
- broad micro-skill coverage,
- skill gates with repeated evidence,
- Exam-level performance,
- speed within target,
- performance on at least two calendar days,
- no critical weak micro-skill,
- passing a closed-book Mastery Challenge.

Hard L4 items are included where reliable generators exist. L5 Expert is optional and never required for badge ownership.

## Psychological design
The learner should be able to replace the vague question “am I ready?” with a concrete state:
- 2/4 badges
- 3/4 badges
- 4/4 → TPA Ready

This is a confidence aid, not a guarantee of passing the official exam.
